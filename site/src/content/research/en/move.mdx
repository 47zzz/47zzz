## Speech carries more than words

MoVE uses a Mixture of Vocalization Experts to preserve non-verbal cues, including laughter and crying, in speech-to-speech translation.

## From one language to another

The system reproduced target vocalizations in 76% of English–Chinese cases using minimal curated data. This first-author work was accepted as a poster at Interspeech 2026.
