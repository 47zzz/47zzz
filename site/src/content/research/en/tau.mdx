## Beyond sound recognition

TAU tests whether audio–language models understand culturally specific soundmarks, beyond identifying their acoustic content.

## Listening in context

The benchmark contains 702 clips and 1,794 questions. It exposes substantial gaps between frontier audio–language models and local listeners. This co-authored work was accepted as a poster at ICASSP 2026.
